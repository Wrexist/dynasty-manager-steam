## About This Game (long description)

```
Build the most powerful football dynasty on the planet — and own the whole
game for one price. No subscription. No ads. No loot boxes. Every feature is
unlocked from the first match.

Take charge of a struggling club buried in the lower divisions. Scout emerging
talent, master tactical formations, survive board pressure, and rise from
obscurity to continental glory across a career that spans decades.

━━━ MANAGE CLUBS ACROSS THE WORLD ━━━
Choose from clubs spanning 45+ divisions across 37 countries — top-flight
European leagues, competitive South American football, Asian powerhouses, and
beyond. Every club fields a genuine squad with realistic ratings, wages, and a
fanbase that expects results. Multi-tier pyramids mean real promotion and
relegation stakes, all the way down.

━━━ BUILD YOUR SQUAD ━━━
Buy, sell, and loan players through a living transfer market with thousands of
targets. Develop talent through specialist training modules, promote youth
academy prospects, and use chemistry links to get the best from every player.
Negotiate contracts, manage the wage bill, and balance the books.

━━━ WIN ON THE PITCH ━━━
A match engine that simulates every one of 90 minutes. Adjust your formation
mid-game, shift mentality, make tactical substitutions, and deliver the
half-time team talk that swings the result. 11 formations, 4 mentalities, and
sliders for tempo, width, pressing, and defensive line. Or sim instantly when
you just want the result — it's included, not paywalled.

━━━ COMPETE FOR EVERY TROPHY ━━━
Win your domestic league. Lift the cups. Qualify for three tiers of continental
competition. Take a national-team job and lead your country through qualifying
to contest international tournaments across five confederations. Every season,
silverware on every front.

━━━ BUILD A MANAGER CAREER ━━━
Create a manager, pitch the board for jobs, earn contracts and bonuses, and
climb — or get sacked and claw your way back. Unlock manager perks, earn
prestige, stock your trophy cabinet, and cement your place in the Hall of
Managers. A deep press-conference system, weekly board objectives, and dynamic
player storylines keep every season fresh.

━━━ OPEN PLAYER PACKS — EARNED, NOT BOUGHT ━━━
Chase your next star through the pack system. On the Steam edition there's no
real-money store: packs are earned with in-game budget and daily free opens,
with cinematic walkout reveals for the big pulls. No gacha spend, ever.

━━━ STEAM FEATURES ━━━
• Steam Achievements across your whole managerial career
• Steam Cloud saves — pick up your dynasty on any PC
• Full keyboard + mouse desktop UI, built for the big screen
• Steam Deck friendly

━━━ ONE PRICE. THE WHOLE GAME. ━━━
Everything is in the box: instant sim, advanced analytics, custom tactics,
the lineup optimizer, the full record book — all of it. No "Pro" tier, no
energy timers, no nagging. Just deep, premium football management.
```
